# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

This project aims to provide a respectful, constructive, and harassment-free environment for everyone participating in the repository and related spaces.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful in discussions, reviews, and issue threads.
- Assume good intent, but give direct and actionable feedback.
- Focus on the code, documentation, benchmarks, and reproducible behavior.
- Welcome different experience levels and technical backgrounds.
- Share context when disagreeing on design or implementation choices.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks
- Trolling, insults, or deliberately disruptive behavior
- Publishing private information without consent
- Abusive language in issues, pull requests, discussions, or reviews

## Enforcement

Repository maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, issues, or pull requests that violate this Code of Conduct. They may also temporarily or permanently restrict participation for repeated or severe violations.

## Reporting

For conduct issues, please contact the maintainers through the support channels listed in [README.md](README.md). Include links, screenshots, or other relevant context where possible.
